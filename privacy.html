Privacy

Short version: K3 never sees your project data.

What happens when you use the builder

The builder is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. Your company details, parties, numbering, logos and settings are processed on your own computer. The Excel files are created on your computer and saved where you choose. Nothing you type or upload is sent to us or to anyone else.

What your browser stores

The builder keeps a draft of your current setup in your browser's local storage, so you don't lose work if you close the tab. It stays on your device. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. We do not add analytics, tracking cookies or advertising.

Feedback

If you send feedback through GitHub Issues, it is public on GitHub under your GitHub account. Don't include confidential project information.
